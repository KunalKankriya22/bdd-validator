Donor List Information

Active Donor List

The active donor list contains donors who are currently eligible for donation. These donors have been matched to the transaction based on key demographic data such as name, date of birth, and gender.

Inactive Donor List

The inactive donor list contains donors who are temporarily or permanently ineligible for donation. These donors may be flagged as inactive due to medical deferrals, frequency restrictions, or other eligibility criteria.

Graded Donor Matching

Donors are matched to transactions using a grading algorithm that compares:

  • Last Name
  • First Name
  • Date of Birth
  • Gender

The grade indicates the quality of the match. Lower grades (closer to 0) indicate better matches. Mismatches in key fields are highlighted in yellow.

Selecting the Right Donor

Review the graded donor list carefully before selecting a donor. Click the eye icon to view full donor details including address and contact information. If the correct donor is not in the list, use the Find Donor feature to search for additional matches.
