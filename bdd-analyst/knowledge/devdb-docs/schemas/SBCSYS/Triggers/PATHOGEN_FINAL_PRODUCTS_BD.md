# PATHOGEN_FINAL_PRODUCTS_BD

**Schema**: [[schemas/SBCSYS/SBCSYS|SBCSYS]]
**Table**: [[schemas/SBCSYS/Tables/PATHOGEN_FINAL_PRODUCTS|PATHOGEN_FINAL_PRODUCTS]]
**Type**: BEFORE EACH ROW
**Event**: DELETE
**Status**: ENABLED

## Trigger Body

```plsql
begin
	insert into PATHOGEN_FINAL_PRODUCTS_AUDIT
	(
		UNIT_ID, PRODUCT_CODE, PROD_WEIGHT,
		PROD_VOL, INSERT_DATE, INSERT_USER,
		PROGRAM_ID,ANTICOAGULANT_VOLUME,
		FINAL_PLT_COUNT,AUDIT_KEY,
		SUIT_PROD_CODE,CAD_PROD_CODE,CHAPTER_ID,
		AUDIT_INSERT_DATS, AUDIT_INSERT_USER

	)
	values
	(

		:OLD.UNIT_ID ,:OLD.PRODUCT_CODE ,
		:OLD.PROD_WEIGHT , :OLD.PROD_VOL ,
		:OLD.INSERT_DATE , :OLD.INSERT_USER ,
		'DELETE' , :OLD.ANTICOAGULANT_VOLUME ,
		:OLD.FINAL_PLT_COUNT , :OLD.AUDIT_KEY ,
		:OLD.SUIT_PROD_CODE , :OLD.CAD_PROD_CODE , :OLD.CHAPTER_ID,
		SYSDATE , lt_user_session.get_userid

	);
end;
```
